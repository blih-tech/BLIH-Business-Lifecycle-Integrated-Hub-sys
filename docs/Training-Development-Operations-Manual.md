# Training & Development Operations Manual

## Executive Overview

### Purpose and Business Objectives

The Training & Development Operations Manual provides comprehensive guidance for managing employee learning and development programs while ensuring strategic alignment, quality delivery, measurable outcomes, and optimal utilization of training resources. This manual serves as the definitive guide for HR teams, department managers, employees, finance controllers, and compliance officers involved in training administration.

### Key Stakeholder Roles and Responsibilities

**Department Managers**: Identify team training needs, approve training participation, support skill application, evaluate training effectiveness, and allocate training resources within budget constraints.

**HR Teams**: Administer training programs, coordinate learning initiatives, manage training budgets, track development progress, and ensure compliance with training requirements.

**Employees**: Participate in training programs, apply learned skills, provide feedback, pursue development opportunities, and maintain personal learning records.

**Finance Controllers**: Approve training budgets, validate training costs, monitor training ROI, ensure financial compliance, and optimize training investments.

**Compliance Officers**: Ensure training meets regulatory requirements, maintain training audit trails, monitor compliance training completion, and address legal obligations.

### Integration with Performance and Succession

Training & Development integrates with performance management to identify skill gaps and development needs, coordinates with succession planning for talent pipeline development, and connects with employee lifecycle for career progression support. This integration ensures comprehensive talent development and organizational capability building.

## Governance & Compliance Framework

### Separation of Duties

**Training Design vs Delivery Separation**: Training program designers cannot be sole deliverers without oversight, ensuring independent quality validation and preventing bias.

**Budget Planning vs Expenditure Approval Separation**: Training budget planners cannot approve expenditures without additional oversight, ensuring proper financial controls.

**Vendor Selection vs Performance Evaluation**: Vendor selection teams cannot conduct performance evaluations without independent review, ensuring objective assessment.

**Policy Administration vs Operational Execution**: Training policy administration is performed separately from day-to-day operational execution, maintaining proper governance and oversight.

### Authority Boundaries

**Manager Training Approval Limits**: Managers can approve team training participation within defined budget limits but cannot override organizational priorities or policy requirements.

**HR Override Capabilities**: HR administrators can modify training processes but cannot override budget approvals or legal compliance requirements.

**Finance Budget Authority**: Finance controllers can validate training costs but cannot modify training priorities or content decisions without proper business justification.

**Executive Approval Thresholds**: Senior executives have final approval authority for training policies but must follow established processes and consider stakeholder input.

### Compliance Controls

**Legal Requirement Compliance**: All training processes comply with employment laws, educational regulations, and contractual obligations, ensuring legal protection and regulatory adherence.

**Documentation Standards**: Proper documentation is maintained for all training decisions, completions, and evaluations, ensuring audit trails and compliance verification.

**Audit Trail Requirements**: Complete records of all training activities are maintained for audit purposes, compliance verification, and process improvement.

**Quality Assurance Mechanisms**: Automated and manual controls ensure consistent application of training standards across all departments and employee levels.
