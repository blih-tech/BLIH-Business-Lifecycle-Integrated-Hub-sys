# Performance Management Operations Manual

## Executive Overview

### Purpose and Business Objectives

The Performance Management Operations Manual provides comprehensive guidance for managing employee performance throughout the organization while ensuring fair evaluation processes, consistent standards, developmental feedback, and alignment with organizational goals. This manual serves as the definitive guide for HR teams, department managers, employees, executives, and compliance officers involved in performance administration.

### Key Stakeholder Roles and Responsibilities

**Department Managers**: Conduct performance evaluations, provide ongoing feedback, set performance goals, develop improvement plans, and make performance-based decisions within established authority levels.

**HR Teams**: Administer performance management processes, ensure policy compliance, coordinate review cycles, provide guidance on evaluation best practices, and handle appeals and exceptions.

**Employees**: Participate in performance evaluations, set personal development goals, seek feedback, document achievements, and engage in performance improvement activities.

**Executives**: Set organizational performance standards, review high-level performance trends, approve performance-related policies, and make strategic performance-based decisions.

**Compliance Officers**: Ensure adherence to performance evaluation regulations, maintain audit trails, monitor fairness and non-discrimination, and address legal obligations.

### Integration with Training and Compensation

Performance management integrates with training systems to identify development needs and track improvement progress, coordinates with compensation for performance-based rewards and adjustments, and connects with employee lifecycle for career planning and succession. This integration ensures consistent performance data flow and supports comprehensive talent management.

## Governance & Compliance Framework

### Separation of Duties

**Evaluation vs Approval Separation**: Managers who conduct performance evaluations cannot approve final ratings without oversight, ensuring independent review and preventing bias.

**Policy Administration vs Operational Execution**: Performance policy administration is performed separately from day-to-day operational execution, maintaining proper controls and preventing conflicts of interest.

**Appeal Review vs Original Evaluation**: Performance appeals are reviewed by different personnel than those who conducted original evaluations, ensuring unbiased assessment.

**Calibration vs Individual Evaluation**: Performance calibration is conducted separately from individual evaluations, maintaining proper oversight and consistency.

### Authority Boundaries

**Manager Evaluation Limits**: Managers can evaluate team performance but cannot override calibration decisions or policy requirements without additional oversight.

**HR Override Capabilities**: HR administrators can modify performance processes but cannot override evaluation outcomes without proper justification and documentation.

**Executive Approval Thresholds**: Senior executives have final approval authority for performance policies but must follow established processes and consider stakeholder input.

**Appeal Authority Limits**: Appeal reviewers can modify ratings but must provide proper justification and follow established appeal procedures.

### Compliance Controls

**Legal Requirement Compliance**: All performance processes comply with employment laws, anti-discrimination regulations, and contractual obligations, ensuring legal protection and regulatory adherence.

**Documentation Standards**: Proper documentation is maintained for all performance decisions, evaluations, and appeals, ensuring audit trails and compliance verification.

**Audit Trail Requirements**: Complete records of all performance activities are maintained for audit purposes, compliance verification, and process improvement.

**Fairness Enforcement Mechanisms**: Automated and manual controls ensure consistent application of performance standards across all departments and employee levels.
