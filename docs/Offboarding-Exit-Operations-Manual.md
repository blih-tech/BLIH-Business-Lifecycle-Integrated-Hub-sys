# Offboarding & Exit Operations Manual

## Executive Overview

### Purpose and Business Objectives

The Offboarding & Exit Operations Manual provides comprehensive guidance for managing employee separations while ensuring smooth transitions, compliance with legal requirements, knowledge transfer, and positive organizational relationships. This manual serves as the definitive guide for HR teams, department managers, employees, finance controllers, IT administrators, and compliance officers involved in offboarding processes.

### Key Stakeholder Roles and Responsibilities

**HR Teams**: Coordinate offboarding processes, ensure compliance with legal requirements, conduct exit interviews, manage final payments, and maintain proper documentation throughout the separation process.

**Department Managers**: Facilitate knowledge transfer, coordinate work handover, provide performance feedback, support team transition, and ensure operational continuity during employee departures.

**Employees**: Participate in offboarding processes, complete required documentation, facilitate knowledge transfer, return company property, and provide constructive feedback through exit interviews.

**Finance Controllers**: Process final payments and settlements, calculate benefits and entitlements, manage financial clearances, ensure tax compliance, and handle compensation-related matters.

**IT Administrators**: Manage system access revocation, recover company equipment, secure data and systems, transfer digital assets, and ensure cybersecurity during offboarding.

**Compliance Officers**: Ensure legal compliance with labor laws, maintain audit trails, verify regulatory adherence, address legal obligations, and mitigate separation-related risks.

### Integration with HR and Payroll

Offboarding & Exit integrates with HR systems for employee record management, coordinates with payroll for final compensation processing, and connects with IT for access and asset management. This integration ensures comprehensive separation management and proper system updates across all organizational functions.

## Resignation Processing

## 1. Use Case Name

## 4. When It Should Be Used

- When employees voluntarily resign from positions
- During notice period management and coordination
- For resignation acceptance and processing

- Resignation must be submitted in writing with proper notice
- Employment contract terms must be reviewed and understood

6. Knowledge transfer schedule is developed and implemented
7. System access and equipment inventory is conducted

## Governance & Compliance Framework

### Separation of Duties

**Termination Decision vs Process Execution**: Termination decision-makers cannot execute offboarding processes without oversight, ensuring independent review and proper procedure adherence.

**Financial Processing vs HR Coordination**: Final payment processors cannot modify offboarding timelines or requirements without HR coordination, maintaining proper process controls.

**Legal Compliance vs Operational Execution**: Legal compliance validation is performed separately from operational offboarding execution, ensuring objective legal review.

**Asset Recovery vs System Administration**: Equipment recovery teams cannot modify system access protocols without IT security oversight, maintaining proper cybersecurity controls.

### Authority Boundaries

**Manager Termination Authority**: Department managers can recommend terminations but cannot execute without HR and legal validation, ensuring proper compliance and risk assessment.

**HR Override Capabilities**: HR administrators can modify offboarding processes but cannot override legal requirements or financial obligations without proper justification.

**Finance Payment Authority**: Finance controllers can validate payment calculations but cannot modify benefits entitlements or legal requirements without proper coordination.

**IT Security Authority**: IT administrators can revoke system access but cannot modify offboarding timelines or legal requirements without proper coordination.

### Compliance Controls

**Legal Requirement Compliance**: All offboarding processes comply with labor laws, employment regulations, and contractual obligations, ensuring legal protection and regulatory adherence.

**Documentation Standards**: Proper documentation is maintained for all separation decisions, processes, and communications, ensuring audit trails and compliance verification.

**Audit Trail Requirements**: Complete records of all offboarding activities are maintained for audit purposes, compliance verification, and process improvement.

**Fair Treatment Enforcement**: Automated and manual controls ensure consistent application of offboarding standards across all employee levels and situations.
